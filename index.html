Why JapNaam

Everything you need for peaceful daily jap

Simple enough for every day. Deep enough for lifelong sadhana. Your counts stay on your device.

  • Jap & Mala Tap to chant with a clear counter and classic 108-bead mala flow.
  • Sadhana analytics Track today’s jap, streaks, sessions, and weekly progress on device.
  • Mantra library Ram, Krishna, Shiva, Hanuman, and more — plus your own mantras.
  • Kids Jap Falling blessings make Naam fun for little devotees, with real jap counts.
  • Stories of bhakti Short devotion stories for inspiration between sessions.
  • Offline & private No account required. Export/import backup. Your jap data stays with you.

How JapNaam works

Open the app, choose your mantra, and begin. Reminders, music, and languages are there when you need them.

  1. Choose a mantra Pick from the library or add your own sacred name.
  2. Start jap Use Jap or Mala mode — or open Kids Jap for play-based chanting.
  3. See your sadhana Check streaks, sessions, and weekly graphs in analytics.
  4. Stay inspired Read stories, set reminders, and jap in Hindi, Gujarati, or English.

Privacy
