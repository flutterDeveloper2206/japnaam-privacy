A calm digital mandir for daily Naam Jap
Package: com.japnaam.application · Version 1.0.0

What is JapNaam?

JapNaam is a peaceful Flutter app for focused Naam Jap. It helps you chant with intention, complete your mala, keep streaks, read devotion stories, and introduce children to Naam through a gentle Kids Jap game — all offline on your device.

No account is required. Your jap history and settings stay on your phone.

Core features

Jap counter Tap to chant with mala tracking (108 beads)
Sadhana analytics Daily target, streaks, sessions, and insights
Mantra library Ram, Krishna, Shiva, Hanuman, and custom mantras
Kids Jap Playful falling blessings — same real jap count
Stories Short bhakti stories for inspiration
Offline first Local storage, export/import backup, no cloud account

Experience details

  • Background mandir music, tap sound, and haptic feedback
  • Daily reminders (optional notifications)
  • Languages: English, Hindi, and Gujarati
  • Light / Dark / Night jap modes
  • Profile avatar and personal display name (optional)

Technology

Built with Flutter and Dart for Android (and iOS-ready). Local data uses on-device storage (Hive). Notifications are handled through the platform notification APIs. The app does not send your jap history to a custom backend.

  • Android package: com.japnaam.application
  • Privacy-first design aligned with Play Console Data safety expectations
  • Store assets prepared for Google Play listing
